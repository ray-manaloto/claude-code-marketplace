## Retrospective barrier and closure

Each unit's retro covers navigation, automated checks, coding standards, global guidance, tool economy, no-op instructions and information access. Mechanical defects use the existing appropriate deterministic check; judgement rules belong in review guidance. Each actionable finding records its owner, affected tickets/paths/checks, exact repair and verification. No-action categories record why no change is needed.

A retro repair is a bounded reviewed change attached to the originating unit's retro record. Its verification and any newly exposed findings stay in that same record; this avoids an infinite chain of retros about retros. The repair itself is allowed through the barrier, while the lane's next product task remains held. Independent review and required checks still apply to repairs.

Affected work includes the originating lane, its relevant dependency descendants, and any open lane whose owned files or required check behavior the repair changes. Direct/dependency successors wait until the repair is accepted. Other affected open lanes may continue isolated implementation but must refresh and recheck before integration or acceptance. Truly independent lanes continue. A shared-file fix owned by the peer is recorded as a handoff with an exact requested outcome and holds only affected work until its reviewed repair is integrated; this task does not edit the peer's files.

Tracker closure is currently a tooling prerequisite, not an available action. The peer owns qualification of the generic acceptance-close operation; this task owns each #11 child's closure decision. Until the operation is integrated and qualified, retain `locally accepted; tracker closure pending`. For each closure, bind the issue/body/dependency preimage, criterion-by-criterion acceptance statement, immutable receipt and reviewed commit, scoped limitations and independent disposition. Verify the state after one journaled mutation; ambiguous outcomes are verify-only. Preserve distinctions such as #76's accepted manual reconciliation after guard rc 2 and #77's hosted fast-check-only acceptance. Never batch-close the 18 merely because they appear in a source-done list.

If a required native-mode, foundation, fresh-session, provider or OCI gate remains unsatisfied, preserve the exact verdict, failed/partial evidence and affected descendants. Continue independent authorized work. When no such work remains, report the graph blocked at the specific gate and present a concrete scope/admission decision; never report the graph complete or silently convert the missing capability into an exception. Retry only with changed evidence or an approved new repair, not as a substitute for a decision. The required LSan chain is an explicit example of this rule.
